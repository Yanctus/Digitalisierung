## SCENE_DESCRIPTION

```
Inside the hold of the sunken ship. At the beginning the space is dark and disconnected: heavy wooden crates, iron fittings and hanging chains sit isolated in still water, each lit only by its own weak violet glow, with no connection between them. Over the course of the shot warm luminous orange light begins to travel along the ship's ribs and beams, finding its way from one object to the next, until every crate and fitting is joined into one continuous glowing structure that follows the shape of the ship itself. Nothing is added, nothing is removed and nothing is rebuilt - only the light spreads along what is already there. At the end the whole hold reads as a single connected system.
```
