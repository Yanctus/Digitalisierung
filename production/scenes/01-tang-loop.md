# 01_TANG_LOOP — die Schleife im Tangwald

Loop-nativ: dasselbe Bild als `first_frame` **und** `last_frame`, dazu die
Anweisung, dass jede Bewegung an ihren Ausgangspunkt zurückkehrt. Die Methode,
die bei der Kuppelhalle 37,7 dB gebracht hat.

- **Start- und Endreferenz:** `01_TANG_MASTER`
- **Kamerablock:** `CAMERA_LOCK`

## SCENE_DESCRIPTION
```
Standing deep inside the kelp forest, looking down the lane between the stems. The shot is a PERFECT SEAMLESS LOOP: it begins and ends on exactly the same image, and every movement returns to its starting position by the end. The kelp blades sway out and come back, particles drift in a slow closed circulation, and the light from above pulses gently and settles again. Nothing enters the frame and nothing leaves it.
```
