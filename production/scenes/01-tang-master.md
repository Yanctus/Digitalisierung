# 01_TANG_MASTER — im Tangwald stehen

Verbindliches Innenraum-Masterbild. Dreifach verwendet: Endframe von
`01_TANG_IN`, Poster, Startframe von `01_TANG_LOOP`.
Stil: `WORLD_STYLE` (v2.0) — derselbe Block wie Welt und Kuppelhalle.

Der Blick, den man hat, **nachdem** man durch den Tang geflogen ist: Man steht
mitten drin, die Halme rechts und links, eine Gasse nach vorn.

## SCENE_DESCRIPTION
```
Standing deep inside a forest of giant kelp on the sea floor, looking straight ahead down a narrow lane between the stems. Towering kelp stalks rise close on the left and on the right, their broad blades drifting slowly. Between them the lane opens into deeper violet water. Old sunken crates and stone tablets lie half buried in the sand at the foot of the stems, each carrying its own small violet glow, none of them connected to any other. A single warm orange lantern rests low in the middle of the lane.
```
